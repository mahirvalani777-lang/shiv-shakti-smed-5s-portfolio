01 / THE BUSINESS CONTEXT

Find the delay.
Design the next move.

01

The operating problem

The workbook describes a 14-minute cylinder search with handwritten labels and no location system, alongside 21 minutes of ethyl/shade adjustment without recorded ink formulas.

SMED ANALYSIS!B14:N14, B21:N21

02

The analytical approach

Separate internal and external work, classify value-added and non-value-added activity, then identify work to externalize, eliminate or reduce. The workbook references Shingo’s SMED theory.

SMED ANALYSIS!B3:N22, B25:E30

03

The management proposal

Pre-stage cylinders, prepare ink during the current run, introduce location codes and recipe cards, and sustain standards with weekly audits.

easy to find.

Proposed practices from the cylinder and ink storage plans. Dashboard status: pending for all seven tracked actions.

Cylinder index

Coded shelves and a register replace memory-based retrieval. Pre-stage the next job’s cylinders while the press runs.

Ink recipe cards

04 / FROM PROPOSAL TO PRACTICE

An eight-week
implementation plan.

Planned windows from the roadmap. No completed milestones or measured post-implementation results are recorded.

WEEKS 1–2

Quick wins

Audit and red-tag cylinders, label active wraps, establish an index, remove empty ink containers and define five zones.

Setter · Supervisor · Operator
WEEKS 2–4

Externalize work

Pre-stage cylinders, prepare ink during the current run and assign old-cylinder cleaning and greasing to the helper.

Setter · Helper
WEEKS 3–6

Standardize

Create recipe cards, label shelf slots, post visual SOPs and train the operator on the pre-stage rule.

Setter · QC · Supervisor
WEEKS 6–8+

Sustain & measure

Run weekly 5S audits, time changeovers against the baseline and update the recipe register monthly.

Supervisor · Setter
